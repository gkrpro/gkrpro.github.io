🤖 AI model security evidence — public dashboard

What this is: A transparency tool. Each cell answers a closed question — "is there a public model card?" "is there a published red-team report?" — with a source link. What it isn't: a ranking, a scoring system, or a recommendation. See the glossary for every term used.

Methodology note: This dashboard catalogues what public evidence exists per model from the source allowlist (provider sites, EU AI Office, Hugging Face, GitHub, AISI, HELM). No model probing. No vendor-comparative claims. Aligned to the AI Model Use Rating Framework v1.0.
● Live-refreshed cells: Columns marked with a small dot — CoP, MC, TD, VD — are auto-fetched on every workflow run (EU AI Office signatory list, Hugging Face model cards, GitHub SECURITY.md). All other cells are manually curated. Cells with the indicator dot in the corner show the auto-verified value; cells without it are manual.

Glossary — acronyms and terms

Every acronym used on this page, defined.

EU regulatory framework
EU AI Act
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — the EU's horizontal AI law. Risk-tiered, with specific obligations for general-purpose models. Enforcement with fines begins 2 August 2026.
GPAI
General-Purpose AI model. EU AI Act classification for models with broad capabilities trained on large data — e.g. most foundation models.
GPAISR
GPAI with Systemic Risk. The Act presumes systemic risk at the ≥10²⁵ FLOP training-compute threshold; the most capable frontier models fall in this band.
CoP
Code of Practice — the voluntary GPAI Code of Practice published by the EU AI Office (July 2025). Full = all chapters; Safety = only Safety & Security chapter; No = not signed.
FLOP
Floating-point operation. Used in the AI Act's compute-based systemic-risk threshold (≥10²⁵ FLOPs during training).
Annex IV
EU AI Act Annex IV — technical-documentation requirements for high-risk and GPAI systems.
Belgian context
CCB
Centre for Cybersecurity Belgium. National cybersecurity authority and CSIRT; receives NIS2 incident notifications.
BIPT
Belgian Institute for Postal Services and Telecommunications. Designated Belgium's main AI Act regulator per the Government Declaration of 31 January 2025.
FOD BOSA
Federal Public Service for Policy and Support. Coordinates AI procurement standards and the AI4Belgium coalition.
GBA / APD
Belgian Data Protection Authority. Publishes AI guidelines under GDPR.
NIS2
Directive (EU) 2022/2555 on cybersecurity. Transposed by Law of 26 April 2024; in force since 18 October 2024. Significant incidents notified to CCB within 24 hours.
CyberFundamentals
CCB's reference framework for demonstrating NIS2 compliance, alongside ISO/IEC 27001.
Security & compliance frameworks
GDPR
General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679. Articles 32 (security) and 35 (DPIA) apply to AI handling personal data.
DPA
Data Processing Agreement — controller-processor contract required by GDPR Article 28.
DPIA
Data Protection Impact Assessment — required under GDPR Article 35 for high-risk processing.
SOC 2
Service Organization Control 2 — AICPA attestation covering security, availability, confidentiality, processing integrity, privacy.
ISO/IEC 27001
International standard for Information Security Management Systems. Recognised by the CCB as a NIS2 reference framework.
ISO/IEC 42001
International standard for AI Management Systems (AIMS), published 2023. First certifiable AI-specific management standard.
CSA CCM
Cloud Security Alliance — Cloud Controls Matrix v4.1. Cloud-specific control framework.
CAIQ
Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire — vendor self-attestation paired with CCM.
MCSB
Microsoft Cloud Security Benchmark v2. Azure-specific technical-implementation guidance.
VDP
Vulnerability Disclosure Policy — public policy for external researchers to report vulnerabilities.
SBOM
Software Bill of Materials — inventory of components, used for supply-chain assurance.
AI-specific frameworks
NIST AI RMF
NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (Jan 2023). Four functions, seven trustworthy-AI characteristics.
NIST AI 600-1
Generative AI Profile of the AI RMF, July 2024. Defines 12 GenAI-specific risk categories.
OWASP LLM Top 10
OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025). LLM01 Prompt Injection, LLM03 Supply Chain, LLM04 Data & Model Poisoning, etc.
AISI
AI Security Institute (UK). Pre-deployment evaluations of frontier models.
HELM
Holistic Evaluation of Language Models (Stanford CRFM). Multi-metric public benchmark suite.
PyRIT
Python Risk Identification Toolkit — Microsoft's open-source AI red-teaming toolkit.
Evidence-column acronyms (table columns)
MC — Model card
Standardised documentation of intended use, training, evaluation, limitations.
SC — System card
Extends model card with deployment-system context (safety mitigations, content filters).
TD — Training data disclosure
Whether training-data origin is publicly described.
SE — Safety evaluation
Whether harm/toxicity/refusal evaluation results are published.
3P — Independent 3rd-party eval
Whether an evaluation by a non-provider party exists publicly.
RT — Red-team report
Whether an adversarial-testing report is publicly available.
S2 — SOC 2
Whether the provider holds a visible SOC 2 attestation.
27 — ISO 27001
Whether the provider holds ISO/IEC 27001 certification.
VD — Vulnerability disclosure
Whether a public VDP or bug-bounty exists.
DPA — Data Processing Agreement
Whether a standard GDPR Article 28 DPA is available.
EU — EU data residency
Whether customer data can be processed in EU-only regions.
NT — No-train clause
Whether enterprise terms include no-training-on-customer-data by default.
Other terms
AI Office
European AI Office, within the European Commission. Enforces GPAI obligations; maintains CoP signatory list.
CSIRT
Computer Security Incident Response Team. In Belgium, the CCB is the national CSIRT.
HF
Hugging Face — open-source model hub where most open-weight models publish.
RAG
Retrieval-Augmented Generation. Architecture combining LLM with external knowledge store.
SaaS
Software-as-a-Service. Here: models accessed via the provider's API.
Open-weight
Model whose weights are publicly downloadable. Note: not necessarily open-source.
